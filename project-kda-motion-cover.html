K/DA - POP/STARS (ft. Madison Beer, (G)I-DLE, Jaira Burns) | Cover by Rain

Motion graphics - Lyric video

As a freelance project I was contacted to create a motion graphics lyric video for a cover of the song “POP/STARS“ by K/DA. I collaborated closely with the performer, Rain, while the cover was being finalised. The objective was to evocate the aesthetic, iconography and general style of the original music video.
