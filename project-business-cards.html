Nimbus Bestiarius

TTRPG Setting Book

Nimbus Bestiarius is a mythological book, consisting on fragments of the original myths of the gods of Nimbus, a fictional world and D&D setting for a personal home game.

The book works as a general introduction both to the fictional world and pantheon. Each god is depicted by an illustration and symbol, amounting to a total of 12.

he complete size of the book is 48 pages, in which it is included the world map of Nimbus, a depiction of the general concept of the setting, various illustrations of their followers, the myths and gods themselves, and the concept art and evolution of the project.

The book was designed, prepared and printed in a 270 x 380 mm hard cover format with gloss paper and reinforced interiors.
