My Own Anthology - Thunderdrop

CD Design

A special collaboration with the composer and musician Thunderdrop. A collection of different soundtracks showing his versatility and style throughout different genres. As such, a design for the entire album (CD, interiors, exteriors and booklet) was needed, showcasing this clash. Collage was the main technique selected.

Overview.

The clash of styles, a grid composition to showcase the different elements. Each one of them a symbol, representing one of the songs of the album.

  • The traditional vs the modern
  • The now vs the past
  • The technological vs the natural world
  • A balance
